# CLAUDE.md

## Working preferences

- **Explain before and after every edit.** Before editing, say what will change and why. After editing, summarise what was changed and the result.
- **Offer several options before edits.** Present multiple approaches, each with an explanation and its trade-offs, and give a recommendation, before making changes.
- **Prefer clean architecture.** Design for maintainability, readability and extensibility: keep layers separated, dependencies pointing inward, and responsibilities small and clear.
